# Decignal design direction

Preserve the warm miniature operational world, the current logo, Inter typography, and chapter colour changes. Improve the existing story; do not replace it with a generic SaaS landing page.

- Palette: background #FBF6EF, paper #FFFDF9, ink #14130F. Cobalt #2E5BFF, violet #7C5CFF, emerald #0FA874, saffron #FFB21E, tangerine #FF7438, pink #FF5FA2. Red #F2361F means risk; emerald means a valid or approved outcome. Source systems retain their existing colours.
- Hierarchy: section headlines 32–60px, weight 620, tracking -0.045em; body 13–17px; labels 9–11px in Geist Mono. One primary statement per scene. Copy names the decision; the object explains it.
- Spacing: 4/8/12/16/24/32/48px increments; content max-width 1152px; page gutters 24px mobile, 40px desktop. Preserve breathing room around the 3D subject while keeping controls and metadata compact.
- Surfaces: quiet hairlines and paper, satin paint, metal, concrete and ribbed plastic. Use rounded corners for buttons and physical objects, not for every section. No decorative glow or unrelated gradient backgrounds.
- Motion: animate cause and effect—stock moves, evidence connects, an object resolves into focus. Applications continue the scroll narrative on a sticky, borderless stage. Signal, context and decision unfold automatically, with continuous scroll progress driving stock movement. Interaction feedback 200–400ms; object positioning uses damped easing. Avoid constant bouncing or motion that competes with reading.
- Product demonstrations: mark examples as illustrative. Every application has a distinct operational object and a concrete signal, evidence and action. Never imply a marketing demo sends an order to a live system.
- Responsive: desktop copy beside a floating scene; mobile copy above the scene. No catalogue panel or required step buttons. Fit the whole model to the available width. Keep text away from the model and controls usable at 390px.
- Verification: inspect desktop, tablet and mobile, keyboard interaction, reduced motion, scroll-driven application progression and policy feedback. Preserve the established theme through every refinement.

Values reflect the repository's tokens and this implementation, not measurements inferred from the reference videos.

## Completed precision pass

Applications use the lead story's detailed vehicles and plants, a finned motor with an exploded bearing inspection, roller conveyors with a physical transfer shuttle, a container yard and alternative route, regional distribution, linked service records and three-way document matching. Scroll progress drives the mechanisms; chapter buttons are optional shortcuts.

The inventory forecast, pallet quantities, retained source stock and arrival timing now agree. Outcomes explain observable changes in those illustrative scenarios rather than unverified performance percentages. The original desktop opening composition remains; phones arrange the six system models in two columns for readable scale.

Audit booking has no configured backend. The form prepares a local downloadable brief and explicitly confirms that nothing was sent. Connect an approved booking or CRM destination before treating it as a live lead form.

## Follow-up clarity pass

Applications now have a named chapter rail and three stage controls that follow scrolling automatically. Stable copy, scene and evidence regions align to shared edges. Eight different instruments show forecasts, demand, capacity, vibration, dependency routes, allocation, case relationships and purchase matching; the action stage changes the evidence rather than repeating static metadata.

Sales uses a separate dealer allocation world. Production equipment includes its tooling, transparent safety glass and controls. The supply-risk world includes a vessel, quay, crane cabin, railings and marked lanes. Cameras fit each subject's proportions, and generic whole-scene rotation has been removed. The navigation remains visible during application stories. The existing entry animation, source colour system, material palette and lead-story mechanics remain the foundation.

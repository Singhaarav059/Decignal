## Implemented refinement

The main story remains the site's lead: systems → signal → shortage → context → transfer → human approval → functions → industries. Supporting paragraphs are shorter and the context includes a compact evidence ledger. Transfer quantities now correspond to retained stock, and the unsupported 34-day cover figure has been replaced with the example's two-day arrival time.

The application section continues the lead story with existing models on a borderless, sticky stage. Inventory, demand, production, maintenance, supply risk, commercial, customer and finance have separate physical scenes. Scrolling automatically unfolds signal, context and decision across all eight applications; stock movement scrubs with scroll progress, and motion communicates movement or a state change. Illustrative labels and concise supporting text keep the demonstration honest.
